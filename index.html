How to Use This Simulator

This webmail security simulator tests your ability to spot social engineering attacks, domain spoofing, and malicious attachments before making a mistake.

  • 25 Email Batch Engine: Starts with 25 emails (40% Phishing, 60% Legitimate) in random order. Automatically, 3 new phishing emails are added every 24 hours.
  • Realistic Inbox Verification & Feedback: If you mark a legitimate email as Safe, a green check tag (✓ Safe) appears next to it. If you mistakenly mark a Phishing email as Safe, an explicit Security Alert Breakdown pops up detailing why you are wrong, and the email receives a permanent ⚠️ Missed Phish tag.
  • Phishing & Spam Routing: When an email is reported as phishing, it gets isolated and moved into the Junk / Spam folder.
  • Persistent User Cache & Retention: All folder states and email selections stay cached per browser. Training Performance stats are retained indefinitely.
